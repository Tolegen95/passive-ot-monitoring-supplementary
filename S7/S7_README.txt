Supplement S7 — QUADAS-2-OT signalling questions and per-report ratings

S7_signalling_questions.csv
  The 15 signalling questions (D1-D4) and the three applicability questions (A1-A3).

S7a_QUADAS-2-OT_signalling_answers_fulltext6.csv
  Answer (Yes / No / Unclear) and basis for each of the 15 signalling questions
  for the six reports re-assessed in full text (W04 [16], W08 [17], W22 [18],
  W25 [19], W31 [20], W36 [21]).

S7b_QUADAS-2-OT_ratings_final.csv
  Final domain ratings (D1-D4), applicability concerns (A1-A3) and their basis
  for all 50 detector-related reports. Ratings for the 12 randomly sampled
  reports include the outcome of second-reviewer adjudication (see S7c);
  every change is recorded in the review_note column. These are the ratings
  used for the counts reported in Section 3.6. The evaluation setting of OT-024
  and W08 [17] was corrected to simulated_or_generated (matching S2 and Table 5),
  and their A1 concern was set to High accordingly.

S7c_second_reviewer_random12_adjudicated.xlsx
  Sheet "Audit": initial rating, independent second-reviewer rating, agreement,
  full-text evidence, adjudication status, final rating and adjudication basis
  for the 48 domain judgements of the 12 randomly sampled reports (seed 20261118).
  Sheet "Summary": 77.1% initial agreement, Cohen's kappa 0.356,
  11 disagreements adjudicated (7 second-reviewer ratings accepted,
  4 initial ratings retained).

Judgement rule (Table 4): Low only if every signalling question in the domain
is answered Yes; High if any answer establishes a specific bias; otherwise Unclear.
